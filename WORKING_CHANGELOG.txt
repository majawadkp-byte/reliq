V1.5 Working — Adaptive Demand Forecasting
- Added 7/30/60/90-day product demand forecasts.
- Added lightweight ridge-regression component using trend, weekly seasonality and annual seasonal signals.
- Added recent 7/30/90-day velocity ensemble and intermittent-demand (Croston-style) component.
- Uses equivalent period from last year when enough history exists, with current-vs-last-year growth adjustment.
- Added demand variability, safety stock, lead-time demand, reorder point, expected stockout date and recommended reorder date.
- Auto-fills Min Stock and Target Stock only for Medium/High confidence forecasts when enabled in Settings.
- MOQ, order multiple and case pack are never modified; they only constrain final suggested order quantity.
- Inventory Intelligence now explains forecast model, confidence score, seasonality, forecast horizons and calculated stock levels.

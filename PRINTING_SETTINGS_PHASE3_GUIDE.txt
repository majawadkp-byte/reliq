RELIQ Solutions V1.4 Working — Printing & Settings Phase 3

PRINTING BEHAVIOR
- Sales invoice / POS receipt, customer cash receipt, purchase invoice/goods receipt, and supplier payment voucher now have independent post-save behavior.
- Options: None, Preview, Print directly.
- Direct printing uses the selected OS printer, or the system default when no explicit printer is selected.
- Sales with no automatic print action show Preview / Print Directly / New Sale after completion.
- Customer receipts and supplier payment vouchers remain manually printable when their automatic action is None.
- Purchase documents can preview/direct-print independently of sales.

SETTINGS REDESIGN
- Printing & Documents is a dedicated section.
- Keyboard helper controls moved to Keyboard & Productivity.
- Settings content is constrained to a readable desktop width and printer controls are responsive.
- Printer list can be refreshed from Settings.

DOCUMENT SETTINGS
- Sales paper format: Thermal 58mm, Thermal 80mm, A4.
- Purchase paper format: Thermal 58mm, Thermal 80mm, A4.
- Barcode label options remain available in Printing & Documents.

IMPORTANT
- Printers must first be installed/configured in Windows or macOS. RELIQ does not install printer drivers.
- Preview creates a PDF and opens it for review; direct printing bypasses the preview.

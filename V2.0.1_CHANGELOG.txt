RELIQ Solutions V2.0.1+201

QUICK WORKFLOW IMPROVEMENTS
- Customer selector in Sales POS is now type-to-search instead of a long dropdown.
- Customer search matches name, phone and email and shows outstanding balance in results.
- Supplier selector in Purchases is now type-to-search by name, phone or email.
- Purchase Order supplier selection uses the same searchable control.
- Smart Buying and Action Center supplier selection are searchable as well.
- Existing searchable customer/supplier selectors in Returns and Accounting Adjustments remain compatible.
- Sales History opens on Today by default with Today, Yesterday, This month and Custom date controls.
- Purchase History uses the same date presets.
- Custom range exposes explicit From and To date buttons.
- Sales History text search now also matches customer phone/email.
- Purchase History text search now also matches supplier phone/email.

DATABASE
- Database schema remains 23. No database migration is required from V2.0.0.

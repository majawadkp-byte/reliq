RELIQ Solutions V1.3 Working - Shortcut Phase 2

ACTIVE CONTEXTUAL SHORTCUTS

Global
- Ctrl/Cmd + K: Command Palette
- Ctrl/Cmd + R: Refresh current page
- Ctrl/Cmd + ,: Settings
- Shift + ?: Keyboard Shortcuts

Navigation
- Alt + 1: Morning Brief
- Alt + 2: Sales / POS
- Alt + 3: Receive Purchase
- Alt + 4: Products & Barcodes
- Alt + 5: Customer Ledgers
- Alt + 6: Supplier Ledgers
- Alt + 7: Payment Activity
- Alt + 8: Reports
- Alt + 9: Business Action Center

Sales / POS
- F2 / Ctrl/Cmd+F: Product / barcode search
- F4: Customer
- F5: Refresh
- F6: Bill discount
- F7: Delivery / charges
- F8: Hold sale
- F9: Payment amount
- F10 / Ctrl/Cmd+S: Complete sale
- Ctrl/Cmd+N: New sale (confirmation if current sale has items)

Receive Purchase
- F2: Supplier
- F3 / Ctrl/Cmd+F: Product search
- F7: Freight
- F9: Payment amount
- F10 / Ctrl/Cmd+S: Receive purchase
- Ctrl/Cmd+N: New purchase (confirmation if current purchase has items)

Products
- F2 / Ctrl/Cmd+F: Search
- F5: Refresh
- Ctrl/Cmd+N: Add product

Stock Adjustment
- F2 / Ctrl/Cmd+F: Search
- F4: Adjust stock
- F5: Refresh history

Customers / Suppliers
- Ctrl/Cmd+F: Search
- Ctrl/Cmd+N: Add new record

SHORTCUT HELPERS
Settings > Sales & POS > Show keyboard shortcut helpers
- ON: shows supported shortcut badges / contextual helper strips.
- OFF: hides visual hints, while keyboard shortcuts remain active.

NOT YET BOUND
Keys such as F11 full-screen and F12 contextual printing are intentionally not forced onto screens that do not yet have a safe matching action/state. They can be added in a later phase once the related workflow is defined.

RELIQ Solutions V1.5 — Adaptive Demand Forecasting

MODEL INPUTS
- Recent 7, 14, 30 and 90-day demand
- Previous comparison periods and recent momentum
- Weekday demand pattern
- Demand variability / safety stock
- Intermittent-demand pattern (Croston-style rate)
- Lightweight ridge regression with trend + weekly + annual seasonal features
- Equivalent future period from last year when sufficient history exists
- Current-vs-last-year growth adjustment
- Supplier lead time
- Current stock and incoming purchase-order stock
- Target coverage days from Settings

OUTPUTS
- 7 / 30 / 60 / 90-day demand forecasts
- Forecast confidence score
- Safety stock
- Automatic minimum stock / reorder point
- Automatic target stock
- Recommended reorder date
- Expected stockout date
- Suggested purchase quantity

AUTOMATIC STOCK LEVELS
Settings → Inventory includes:
1. Auto-fill Minimum Stock & Target Stock from demand forecast
2. Use last-year seasonality when available
3. Default target stock coverage (days)

Auto-fill only writes Min Stock and Target Stock when forecast confidence is Medium or High. Products with limited history retain their configured values.

MOQ
Forecasting does not change MOQ, order multiple or case pack. Existing purchasing constraints are applied after demand forecasting to round the recommended purchase quantity.

SEASONALITY
When approximately a year of history exists, RELIQ compares the equivalent period from last year with recent demand and blends it into the forecast. This is intended to capture recurring seasonal peaks/dips without letting last year's pattern override current trading conditions.

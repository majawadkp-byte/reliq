RELIQ Solutions V1.6 — Product Lifecycle, Product Types & Forecast Continuity

NEW PRODUCT TYPES
- Stocked Product: quantity tracked; included in stock valuation, stock alerts, reorder logic and demand-stock forecasting.
- Non-stocked Product: physical item that can be sold/purchased but does not maintain on-hand quantity.
- Service: no stock movement; can be sold and/or purchased; excluded from inventory forecasting.
- Recipe / Combo: sellable parent item whose availability/consumption is based on configured Stocked components.

SELLABLE / PURCHASABLE
- Sellable controls whether an active product is available in Sales POS.
- Purchasable controls whether an active product is available in Purchase Entry.
- These controls are independent of product type.

PRODUCT LIFECYCLE
- Active: normal operational product.
- Discontinued: retained for history but removed from normal operational lists.
- Replaced: retained for history and linked to a replacement product.
- Archived: retained for historical integrity and hidden from normal operations.

SAFE DELETE
- Products with no stock and no transaction/reference history can be permanently deleted.
- Products with stock, sales, purchases, returns, stock movement, PO, transfer, stock-count or recipe history are archived instead of hard-deleted.

REPLACEMENT / DEMAND FAMILY
- A discontinued product can point to its replacement.
- Demand Family is an optional equivalent-product grouping, e.g. "13mm Cordless Drill".
- Forecasting does not blindly combine active substitutes.

FORECAST MODEL ADJUSTMENT
- Replacement products can inherit predecessor history for cold-start forecasting.
- Predecessor contribution automatically reduces as the new product gets its own transactions:
  <5 own transactions: 85% predecessor weight
  <15: 60%
  <30: 35%
  30+: 15%
- When there is no explicit predecessor and own history is sparse, discontinued members of the same Demand Family can provide a low-weight support signal.
- Last-year seasonality, weekday effects, trend, intermittent-demand handling, variability, ridge regression and lead-time safety stock remain part of the ensemble.
- MOQ, order multiple and case pack are never changed by the model.

SEARCHABLE SELECTORS
- Recipe / Combo component selection now supports typing product name, SKU or barcode.
- The same searchable selector is used in the purchase-line recipe/component editor.
- Product replacement selection is searchable.
- Customer/Supplier selection in unlinked returns is searchable.
- Stock Adjustment and purchase/sales product lookup remain searchable.

IMPORT / MIGRATION
- Product bulk import supports Service and Non-stocked types plus sellable, purchasable, lifecycle status, demand family and history-inheritance fields.
- Full Migration product CSV supports the same lifecycle/product-type fields.

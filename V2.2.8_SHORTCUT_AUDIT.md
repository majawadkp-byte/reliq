# RELIQ V2.2.8 Keyboard Shortcut Audit

## Global shell
- Cmd/Ctrl + K — Command Palette
- Cmd/Ctrl + F — Context search: Customer/Supplier search when those ledger tabs are active; otherwise Product Lookup
- Cmd/Ctrl + R — Refresh current screen
- Cmd/Ctrl + , — Settings
- Shift + ? — Keyboard Shortcuts
- Alt + 1 — Morning Brief
- Alt + 2 — Sales / POS
- Alt + 3 — Receive Purchase
- Alt + 4 — Products & Barcodes
- Alt + 5 — Customer Ledgers
- Alt + 6 — Supplier Ledgers
- Alt + 7 — Payment Activity
- Alt + 8 — Reports
- Alt + 9 — Business Action Center

## Sales / POS
- F2 — Product/barcode search
- F4 — Customer selection
- F5 — Refresh POS
- F6 — Bill discount
- F7 — Delivery/charges
- F8 — Hold sale
- F9 — Payment amount
- F10 — Complete sale
- Cmd/Ctrl + N — New sale
- Cmd/Ctrl + S — Complete sale
- Cmd/Ctrl + F — Product/barcode search

## Purchases
- F2 — Supplier selection
- F3 — Product lookup
- F7 — Freight/delivery
- F9 — Payment amount
- F10 — Receive/save purchase
- Cmd/Ctrl + N — New purchase
- Cmd/Ctrl + S — Receive/save purchase
- Cmd/Ctrl + F — Product lookup

## Products / Stock
- Products: F2 / Cmd/Ctrl + F — Product search
- Products: F5 — Refresh
- Products: Cmd/Ctrl + N — New product
- Stock Adjustment: F2 / Cmd/Ctrl + F — Search
- Stock Adjustment: F4 — Pick and adjust
- Stock Adjustment: F5 — Refresh

## Customers / Suppliers
- Cmd/Ctrl + F — Focus active customer/supplier search
- Cmd/Ctrl + N — Add customer/supplier

## Notes
- Escape behavior for dialogs/menus remains provided by Flutter/macOS/Windows navigation semantics rather than a destructive global root shortcut.
- Shortcut helper visibility in Settings does not disable the shortcuts themselves.

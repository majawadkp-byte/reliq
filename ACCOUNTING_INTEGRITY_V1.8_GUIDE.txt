RELIQ Solutions V1.8 — Accounting Integrity II
================================================

This working release adds:

1. Unapplied payment allocation
   - Customer receipts and supplier payments can retain unused account credit/advance.
   - The Unapplied Credits workspace shows the remaining amount on the original payment.
   - Allocate later against open invoices, bills or opening-balance documents.
   - RELIQ preserves the original payment ID and creates allocation rows instead of duplicating cash movement.

2. Opening balances and account adjustments
   Customer:
   - Opening Receivable: customer owes the business.
   - Opening Credit: business already owes/holds credit for the customer.
   - Customer Credit Note: reduces receivable; excess becomes customer credit.
   - Customer Debit Note: increases receivable.

   Supplier:
   - Opening Payable: business owes supplier.
   - Opening Advance: supplier holds an advance/credit for the business.
   - Supplier Debit Note: reduces payable; excess becomes supplier advance.
   - Supplier Credit Note: increases payable.

3. Aging integration
   - Open adjustment documents are included with invoices/bills in receivable/payable aging.
   - Payments can be allocated to adjustment/opening documents as well as normal invoices.

4. Accounting integrity controls
   - Compares customer/supplier master balances with the total of open invoices/bills + adjustments.
   - Shows customer credits, supplier advances and unapplied payments.
   - Authorized users can rebuild party balances from open documents if a historical import/correction causes drift.
   - Rebuild does not modify account credits/advances.

5. Payment reconciliation
   - Payments can be marked reconciled against Bank, Cash, Card Clearing or Other.
   - Optional statement/deposit reference and notes.
   - Reconciled/unreconciled totals and filters.
   - Reconciliation changes are written to the audit trail.

6. Permissions
   - accounting_adjustments: opening balances, credit/debit notes, unapplied allocations and balance rebuild.
   - reconciliation: payment reconciliation.
   - Admin and Accountant receive both by default; legacy Manager remains compatible.

Database schema: v21 / RELIQ 1.8.0
